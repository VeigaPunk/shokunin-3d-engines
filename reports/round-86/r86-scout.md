**The boundary is not line count and not byte size.** 2 026 lines of blank lines
or 256 KB of comment (30× the fixture's r85 threshold in bytes, 3× in lines)
never flip it; 625 lines carrying 600 real statements, or 600 functions, or 600
class members, or a 256 KB string literal do. The discriminator is **how much
work the script forces the engine to do while starting up**, i.e. *when the
first deferred flush falls in the frame* — every null case printed
`pf=1`/`first_ok_tick=1` and every non-null case printed `pf=2`, i.e. the
deferred `_run` body ran after the main thread had already entered a physics
phase. `ord.gd` (2 reps, threaded and DEFAULT) shows the fetch position itself
is unchanged (`run_first pf=0` in all six runs) — the *flush point* moves, not
the fetch position.

**Minimum reproducer (measured):** `sz_none0.gd`, **26 lines / 639 B** → null at
position B under `proj_thr`, 2/2. r85's preamble-length result is reproduced on
an independent script: `introspect.gd` (88 lines) is null at B on `proj_thr`
and `proj_jolt_thr` (1 rep each, both hang — §2) and non-null on
`proj`/`proj_jolt`/`proj_dummy` (1 rep each).

**Self-guard — measured and it works.** The ladder template's bounded retry
(`for i in range(24): await physics_frame; if fetch != null: break`) reported
`first_ok_tick=1` in **58/58 runs** across all 29 ladder probes, and
`RETRY first_ok_tick=1` in all four `thr_fetch` variants. So **one physics
frame always suffices**; a guard of the form "fetch after `await physics_frame`
(one retry), fail named if still null" makes the fixture size-independent. The
landed bare assert (`phys3d_events.gd:333-334`) is correct on today's 1196-line
fixture (11 arms × 2 reps under `proj_thr` all non-null) but is a *size*
dependency, and an unguarded deref of the null is a hang (§3).

3. Every new arm must (a) Variant-type + null-check every server read, and (b)
   never deref a possibly-null direct state. Both are cheap; the guarded
   counterpart exits rc 1 in <0.5 s.
