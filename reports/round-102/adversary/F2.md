## 3. Deletion would be inconsistent with the lane's own doctrine — the pointer *is* the durable record

`corpus/live/` is a deliberate, documented convention, not an accident. The lane's own closure text states the rule:

> "Ignored `live/runs/` directories are not durable evidence; committed review and reproduction commands are the pointer."

That sentence is the crux. Under house doctrine, the ignored receipt is *expected* to be ephemeral; the **committed lesson line is by definition the durable pointer to it**.
