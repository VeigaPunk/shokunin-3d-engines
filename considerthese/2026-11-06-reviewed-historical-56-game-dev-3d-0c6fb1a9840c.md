# 3D: stability is not motion fidelity

[Stam](https://www.josstam.com/_files/ugd/cf1fd6_898fe9b63df946689101b8d074f8efba.pdf) combines backward tracing and linear interpolation with implicit diffusion and projection. Bounded transport does not establish physical accuracy; the baseline remains useful for inspecting dissipation.

[Fedkiw, Stam and Jensen](https://physbam.stanford.edu/papers/stanford2001-01.pdf) adapt earlier CFD vorticity confinement to counter coarse-grid dissipation. Stability requires the forcing magnitude below a threshold. Staggered velocity placement and interpolation also differ, so this is not simply one added control. Shared authorship is not independent validation.
