# Hair scattering models

Marschner et al., [Light Scattering from Human Hair Fibers](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~srm/publications/SG03-hair.pdf), SIGGRAPH 2003, §§1,2.1,6–7: surface reflection, transmission and internal reflection explain distinct contributions. Their rendered images omit scattering between hairs.

d’Eon et al., [An Energy-Conserving Hair Reflectance Model](https://eugenedeon.com/pdfs/egsrhair.pdf), EGSR 2011, §§3–6: revisits roughness normalization and additional internal paths; cuticle tilt remains approximate. These optical studies establish no appearance or recognition outcome for the original fictional disguise.
