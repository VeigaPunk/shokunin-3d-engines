# 3D: camera control and composition

[Drucker–Zeltzer](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/gimuseum_wfigs.pdf): selected manuscript sections separate camera modules from interface widgets. State, initialization, controller and constraints organize task-specific control. Continuity of control differs from continuity of a shot.

[Lino–Christie](https://cinematography.inria.fr/files/2015/03/toric-space-tog-final.pdf): Toric coordinates and interval pruning organize target-relative composition requirements. Sampled candidate viewpoints evaluate visibility through rays to bounding-box corners and center. Interpolation and interactive manipulation explicitly do not check visibility. Handling two targets and unresolved extensions remain material limits; favorable endpoints do not guarantee an unobstructed transition.
