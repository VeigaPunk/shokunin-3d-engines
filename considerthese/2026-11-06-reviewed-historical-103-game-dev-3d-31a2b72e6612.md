# Texture synthesis: pixels to patches

Efros/Leung, [Texture Synthesis by Non-parametric Sampling](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~dyer/cs534/papers/efros-leung-iccv99.pdf), ICCV 1999, §§1–4: grows texture pixelwise from matching neighborhoods. Limited context can produce drift or verbatim copying. Retained Wisconsin manuscript: six pages; January 2003 PDF metadata is not publication dating.

Efros/Freeman, [Image Quilting for Texture Synthesis and Transfer](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~efros/research/quilting/quilting.pdf), 2001, §§1.2–2.3: replaces pixelwise growth with overlapping patches and minimum-error boundaries. Repetition and distorted joins persist. Retained Berkeley manuscript: six pages, May 2001 PDF metadata; SIGGRAPH attribution comes from the historical author-project check, not this manuscript's imprint.

This progression concerns image texture synthesis, not spatial assembly or guaranteed production performance. Publisher byte identity and unrestricted reuse or training permission are unestablished.
