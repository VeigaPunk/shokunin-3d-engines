# Geometric quadrics and correlated attributes

Garland and Heckbert, [Geometric quadrics](https://www.mgarland.org/files/papers/quadrics.pdf), SIGGRAPH 1997, §§5,8: plane distances approximate geometric error locally. Continuous colour attributes remained future work.

Their [Attribute extension](https://www.mgarland.org/files/papers/quadric2.pdf), 1998, §§5–6.1: joint quadrics retain position/attribute correlations for freely placed replacement vertices. Continuous linear/Euclidean attributes are assumed; general multiple values remain unimplemented. This distinguishes joint error treatment from simply placing separate geometric and attribute costs beside each other.

Eight-page author manuscripts; exact final proceedings editions unverified. August 1997 and July 1998 file metadata are not publication dates. Unrestricted reuse unestablished. Earlier reduction/seam notes remain separate context; this comparison concerns correlated attributes under unconstrained placement.

Optional exercises omitted; originals retained.
