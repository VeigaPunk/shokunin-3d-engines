## Callback evidence and limits

The SceneTree `pathfollow3d_callback.gd` authors the three-point bent `Curve3D` with middle point `(2,2,1)`, handles `(-0.5,0,-0.25)`/`(0.5,0,0.25)`, baked length≈6.072724. It adds a `Path3D`, child `PathFollow3D` with `ROTATION_NONE`, and follower-child marker `Node3D` at local Z+1. It attaches a separate live `Node3D` instance from `pathfollow3d_driver.gd`, assigns its follower/marker references and stationary flag **before** adding the driver to the root, then awaits 65 physics-frame signals and reads the live setup, callback counters, delta sum, progress and captured positions. The SceneTree script never writes follower progress in its moving phase (indeed it never assigns it). The driver has actual `func _physics_process(delta: float)`: while `ticks < 60`, the moving arm adds `SPEED * delta` to `follower.progress`, both arms add delta to `delta_sum` and increment ticks, and the driver captures live follower global position at callbacks 30/60 and marker global position at callback 60. Subsequent callbacks return without increment or motion. Source `project.godot` pins physics to 60Hz, so observed 60 callback deltas sum≈1.0 (tolerance 0.003), giving moving progress≈3.0 from `SPEED=3.0` (tolerance 0.003). The 65 awaited signals provide a bounded observation window, and the runner bounds case time to 20 seconds; the asserted callback budget is exactly 60, not a claim that 65 signals equal 65 callbacks.

The moving arm measures first follower≈`(0.906026,1.105767,0.453013)` at callback30, second≈`(1.968047,1.997535,0.984023)` at callback60, and marker at measured second position plus Z1, all within tolerance 0.003. Same live geometry and **same 60 callback count and delta-sum guard** apply to stationary speed0 control; its progress and follower samples stay at origin and marker remains `(0,0,1)` within 0.0001. Live node/parent/reference and baked-length setup guards precede measured callback count/delta and progress/position assertions. The positive progress, first/second position and marker position checks are a compound predicate, not individually mutation-isolated.
